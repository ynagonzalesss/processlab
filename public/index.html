An AI that knows when not to use AI.

ProcessLab maps every step of a business process to one of five lanes, from "keep as-is" to "human only", and explains why. Click any step and change its traits to watch the engine re-decide in real time.

The LLM interprets. The rules engine decides.
R1 already automated → KEEP
R2 structured + clear rule → AUTOMATE
R3 irreversible / high stakes → HUMAN
R4 messy, low stakes, reversible → AI
R5 messy with real stakes → AI + HUMAN
